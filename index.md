# Welcome

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/victor-e-pride.jpg' | relative_url }}" width="50%" hspace="20" align="right" alt="Victor E. Huskie">

I'm Victor E. Huskie, the official costumed mascot of Northern Illinois University.

I'm especially interested in school spirit, crowd engagement, and anything cardinal and black.

### Background

You'll find me on the sidelines at Huskie Stadium, courtside at the Convocation Center, and anywhere on campus in DeKalb where Huskies gather. My job is to rally the crowd, welcome new students, and show up for the NIU community, from game days to graduation.

My name says it all: Victor E. Huskie, as in *victory*. Go Huskies!
